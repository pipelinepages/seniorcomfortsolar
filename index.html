Important: not every heavy appliance should run at once.

The system is designed around realistic simultaneous use. High-power appliances such as a washing machine, water pump, electric kettle and microwave should not all be assumed to operate at the same time. Homes with multiple air conditioners, electric cooking, water heating or heavier loads may need a 7.5–10kW inverter, approximately 15–20kWh of lithium storage and a larger solar array.

Frequently asked questions

Before you choose a package.

Will my parent need to switch anything manually?

The proposal includes automatic solar/battery/grid changeover. The design objective is to keep normal operation simple enough that the senior occupant does not need to manage the system.

Can it power air conditioners?

The standard listed packages are built around the stated essential loads. Homes with air conditioners or other heavy loads should be assessed for an upgraded system.

Can family members monitor the system remotely?

Remote inverter and battery monitoring is included where the selected equipment supports it.

What happens when there is little solar energy?

The battery can supply the home automatically when solar production is insufficient, while the grid remains available as an additional backup source.

Is the electrical installation protected?

The proposal specifies proper cable sizing, earthing, over-current protection, surge protection, battery isolation and manufacturer-compliant settings.

How do I know which package is right?

A site load audit should be completed before final equipment selection so the system reflects the home's real appliance use.
